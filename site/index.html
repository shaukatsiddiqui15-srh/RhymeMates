What you should know

Rhyme Mates is made by a small independent team. Here is how we think about what your child watches.

⏱

Short on purpose

Every video runs one to three minutes. Long enough to finish a song, short enough to stop after one.

📚

Built around learning

Letters, numbers, colours, shapes, opposites, the five senses, weather and days of the week — wrapped in a tune that repeats.

🪔

English and हिंदी

Traditional Hindi rhymes sit alongside English originals, so children can hear both languages sung the same way.

🔒

No tracking on this site

This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics. YouTube is only contacted once you press play on a video.

👶

Made for Kids

Every upload is marked Made for Kids on YouTube, which disables personalised ads and comments on the channel.

🎵

Original songs

The melodies, lyrics and characters are ours. Traditional rhymes are re-recorded rather than reused.

New rhymes every week
